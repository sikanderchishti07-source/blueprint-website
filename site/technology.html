Licences & accreditations

Accredited to do the work that counts

Studies and permit files are only accepted from accredited parties. BluePrint operates under full regulatory licensing in the Kingdom, holding active registration and accreditation from the following authorities.

Environmental Consulting & Services Licence

National Center for Environmental Compliance (NCEC)

Licensed across more than 20 environmental activities, from studies and consulting to remediation.

Approved Environmental Service Provider

Royal Commission for Jubail & Yanbu

Approved to work inside the Kingdom’s two largest industrial cities.

Waste Management Service Provider

National Center for Waste Management (MWAN)

Registered for industrial and hazardous waste treatment, sorting, recycling and disposal.

IAS Accreditation

International Accreditation Service

International accreditation covering our laboratory and inspection work.

ISO 9001:2015

Quality Management

A certified quality system behind every study, report and submission.

ISO 14001

Environmental Management

We hold ourselves to the same environmental management standard we advise on.

ISO 45001:2018

Occupational Health & Safety

Certified safety management for field, laboratory and site work.

Licensed across 20+ environmental activities

BluePrint is licensed and approved by the NCEC, the National Center for Waste Management (MWAN) and the Royal Commission for Jubail & Yanbu — spanning environmental studies and consulting, sustainability and corporate governance, industrial and hazardous waste management, landfill construction and lining, emergency response and site remediation.
