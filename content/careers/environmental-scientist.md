## The role

You will carry out environmental monitoring campaigns, contribute to impact
assessments and prepare the reports that clients submit to the regulator.
The work is a mix of field days, laboratory liaison and desk-based reporting.

## What you will do

- Plan and carry out air, water, noise and soil monitoring campaigns
- Prepare sampling plans and maintain chain-of-custody records
- Interpret laboratory results against the limits that apply to each client
- Draft impact assessments and periodic compliance reports
- Liaise with regulators on submitted files

## What we are looking for

- A degree in environmental science, environmental engineering or a related field
- Familiarity with the Saudi environmental framework, or a willingness to learn it properly
- Careful record-keeping — in this work the documentation matters as much as the measurement
- A valid Saudi driving licence, since field work means travel
- Arabic and English

## What we offer

Varied work across coastal, desert and industrial sites, practice carried out
under accreditation, and genuine depth in a regulatory framework that is still
taking shape.
